PaceTrace

// designer & developer //

An iPhone app that turns Apple Health workout data into a searchable history, trend dashboards, and clean exports.

PaceTrace reads workouts from Apple Health and keeps them in one place: running, cycling, swimming, hiking, and core training, with filters for each sport.

The demo walks through the four tabs: the workout list, the dashboard, export, and settings.

how it works

workouts

Every workout from Apple Health appears in a single list with its duration, distance or energy, and date. Filter chips narrow it to one sport.

dashboard

Each sport gets a trend card that compares the last period to the one before it, with a label for whether you’re improving or declining. Running and cycling use an efficiency factor, which is speed per heartbeat on aerobic-zone sessions. Swimming tracks pace per 100 m, and hiking tracks heart rate on comparable climbs. The time range runs from one month to all time.

export

Pick a date range and a format (XLSX, CSV, or TSV), and PaceTrace builds the files. The Excel workbook has five sheets: All, Swim, Bike, Run, and Other.

settings

Set your critical swim speed, birth year or tested max heart rate, and display units. The dashboard uses these to find aerobic-zone workouts.

hello, I’m renee

I’m a creative researcher who puts people first. With a Ph.D. in Biological Anthropology and 9+ years of research experience, I design studies and help other researchers design and run theirs.

My work spans health technology, clinical research, and consumer products. I’ve designed multi-year, multi-phase studies, coordinated IRB- and FDA-compliant clinical research, supported UXR studies from scoping to insight, and built documentation and AI-assisted workflows that save teams time.

I care about public health and about people. Before health tech, I studied how bodies record diet, movement, and health, from wild chimpanzees to women’s health screening. I’m drawn to women’s health topics that don’t get talked about enough, from periods and pelvic floor health to heart health after menopause, and I helped create and validate a screening tool for a common but under-discussed condition in female athletes and active women. Outside of work, I’m an endurance athlete: I move in nature (backpacking, rock climbing), train for triathlons, and race endurance events, including Ironman 70.3, Olympic-distance triathlons, and many running races.

Wherever I work, I try to be the teammate who builds others up and makes someone smile. I value collaboration and a little levity, because people do their best thinking when they feel encouraged and supported by their team.

I hope to keep building toward health products that reach more people and contribute to products that support global health.
